# Privacy, truthfulness, and safe handling

## Personal data

- Process only information needed for the requested task.
- Keep source resumes outside the skill package.
- Preserve original files and avoid silent overwrite.
- Redact phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, identity numbers, compensation, and private links from public examples.
- Do not add personal data or one-off employer research to a shared knowledge base without explicit permission.

## Resume integrity

- Never invent employment, education, dates, titles, metrics, clients, tools, certifications, or outcomes.
- Label estimates and ranges accurately.
- Keep confidential figures generalized when needed.
- Separate individual contribution from team, company, brand, platform, and market effects.
- Treat an unexplained inconsistency as a question, not proof of dishonesty.

## Employer research

- Use public, lawful sources.
- Do not expose credentials, session cookies, private documents, or paid database content.
- Distinguish fact, inference, unknown, and conflict.
- Do not guarantee employer safety, future funding, job security, or investment value.
- Do not rely on protected characteristics when assessing role fit or career value.

## High-stakes boundaries

Provide general informational guidance, not definitive legal, tax, immigration, medical, or investment advice. Identify when a qualified professional is necessary.
