# Career and offer decisions

## Start from the job seeker

Establish current stage, target direction, strongest transferable evidence, missing capability, compensation floor, location, family constraints, risk tolerance, and desired time horizon. Mark missing preferences as unknown; do not invent them.

## Identify the next career asset

Determine which asset matters most over the next two to three years:

- platform or brand credibility;
- functional depth;
- industry depth;
- zero-to-one ownership;
- scale and operational complexity;
- people management and organizational authority;
- commercialization, profit, or full-business responsibility;
- international exposure, language, or regulated-domain experience.

Judge opportunities by whether they build the needed asset with credible scope and evidence.

## Compare opportunities

Evaluate:

1. Role value: mission, scope, authority, reporting line, visibility, and success metrics.
2. Company value: business quality, stage, stability, and strategic importance of the team.
3. Growth value: capabilities and credible stories accumulated in two to three years.
4. Return: guaranteed cash, variable pay, equity, benefits, and likely working hours.
5. Risk cost: layoffs, business failure, leadership instability, commute, relocation, health, and family constraints.
6. Exit path: future employers, roles, seniority, and ability to explain the move.

Do not let a total score hide a red line. Use conditional advice: choose A for one goal and B for another.

## Recommendation labels

- Accept: evidence supports the move and no unresolved red line remains.
- Negotiate then accept: value is positive but written terms or scope must change.
- Hold: material facts remain unknown.
- Decline: the opportunity fails a central career goal or crosses a red line.

## Negotiation and verification

Separate facts that must be written from facts that can remain conversational. Confirm title, legal employer, base salary, variable-pay formula, probation terms, location, reporting line, equity instrument, vesting, exercise rules, restrictive covenants, and start date when relevant. Recommend qualified legal, tax, or immigration advice when those details determine the choice.

## Output

Lead with the recommendation. Explain the decisive career asset, the main downside, the two-to-three-year exit, and at most three unknowns that could change the decision.
