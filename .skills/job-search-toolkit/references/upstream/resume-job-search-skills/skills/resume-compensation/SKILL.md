# Compensation

## Procedure

1. Research the role, level, location, company disclosures, and current market sources. Treat self-reported data as directional.
2. Separate base salary, bonus, commission, equity, signing or retention payments, benefits, and work conditions.
3. Define the candidate's target, acceptable floor, walk-away conditions, and strongest evidence of rapid value.
4. When asked early, first request the budgeted band or typical offer because responsibilities and level may still be unclear.
5. If a number is required, give a researched range and state that the complete package and role scope matter.
6. Do not reveal salary history unless the candidate intentionally chooses to and it is appropriate for the jurisdiction.
7. Evaluate the written offer as a whole; identify what is fixed, what may be negotiable, and what needs clarification.

## Boundary

Current compensation rules and disclosure laws vary. Verify the relevant jurisdiction before making a legal claim. Never fabricate a competing offer.
