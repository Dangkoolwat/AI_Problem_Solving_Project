# Employer Due Diligence

Ask for real examples rather than opinions or promises.

## Question bank

Choose three or four that match the user's risks and interview stage:

- Tell me about someone on the team whose career has grown. What did that look like?
- Tell me about a time someone on the team was struggling. How did you approach it?
- Describe a recent decision that affected the team. How did you communicate it and get input?
- Tell me about a time the team disagreed with leadership. How did you handle it?
- What does constructive feedback look like here? Can you share a recent example?
- Tell me about a period when the team was stretched thin. How did you manage workload?
- In a final conversation: what is the most common reason people leave this team or do not make it through the first year?
- What measurable outcomes would exceed expectations in the first six months?

## Evaluation

Listen for specific examples, ownership, coaching, early feedback, prioritization, advocacy, honest trade-offs, and self-awareness. Treat vague “open door” or “great culture” claims as unproven, not automatically false. Record the answer, evidence, concern, and follow-up before deciding.
