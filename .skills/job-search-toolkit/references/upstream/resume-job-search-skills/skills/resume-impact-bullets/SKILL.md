# Impact Bullets

Use the structure that best fits the available evidence, not a forced formula.

## Structures

- **XYZ:** Accomplished X, measured by Y, by doing Z.
- **Outcome first:** Delivered outcome; explain the action, scope, and relevant constraint.
- **HERO for senior work:** Headline outcome → Effect on business/users → Rationale and trade-offs → Operations/execution.
- **Proof without a metric:** Improved a named condition through a specific action, with scope, adoption, decision, or external validation.

## Procedure

1. Identify the verified result, candidate contribution, method, scale, and reason the work mattered.
2. Lead with an ownership verb only when the ledger supports it. Prefer `led`, `drove`, `owned`, `launched`, or `designed` over `helped` only when accurate.
3. Translate internal jargon into language a reader outside the company understands.
4. Show a balanced signal across execution, business/user impact, and leadership/judgment when the evidence supports all three.
5. Remove generic baselines such as “detail-oriented” or “excellent communicator”; demonstrate them through outcomes.
6. If a required element is unknown, leave a bracketed evidence request instead of filling it.

## Quality test

A reader should be able to answer: What changed? What did this person do? Why did it matter? What evidence supports the claim?
