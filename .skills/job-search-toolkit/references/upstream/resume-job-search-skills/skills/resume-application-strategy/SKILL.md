# Application Strategy

## Procedure

1. Define one role family, acceptable constraints, target companies, and stop conditions.
2. Apply the role-targeting gate before spending time on materials. Skip genuine hard blockers.
3. Use a multi-channel mix:
   - current public roles as the primary market;
   - existing warm relationships and targeted outreach;
   - recruiter-search visibility through LinkedIn;
   - selective direct outreach where the candidate has specific relevant proof.
4. Apply early when a well-matched role appears, but do not confuse speed with fit.
5. Track source, posting date, application date, resume version, contacts, stage, next follow-up, and outcome.
6. After real conversation begins, ask for the decision timeline. If it passes without an answer, follow up once, then keep moving.
7. Treat rejection and silence as noisy pipeline signals, not proof of personal or ATS failure. Preserve a professional exit because teams revisit prior candidates.
8. Review conversion by stage. Change positioning when qualified applications do not produce screens; change interview preparation when screens do not produce later rounds.

## Stop rules

Do not chase a mythical hidden market, buy unsupported match scores, or send hundreds of undifferentiated applications. Do not regenerate a resume when the posting and evidence have not materially changed.
