# Cover Letter

## Procedure

1. Read the current company source and job description; do not rely on model memory for mission, values, or current priorities.
2. Rank the role's core business problems and choose two or three verified candidate examples that answer them.
3. Draft in layers:
   - why this problem and role;
   - relevant evidence and outcomes;
   - how the candidate would contribute;
   - concise close.
4. Use company language only when accurate and natural. Avoid copying the posting or opening with generic admiration.
5. Keep the tone consistent with the candidate's real voice and resume.
6. Run a claim-ledger check and remove generated metrics, tools, or facts that were not supplied.

## Output

Deliver the letter plus a short claim map and any company fact that still needs verification. A generated first draft is not submission-ready until the candidate facts and company facts are checked.
